## RESTRICTED DIETS FOR CYBERNETIC RECIPIENTS

Nutrition plays a surprisingly critical role in the acceptance of cybernetic implants. A patient's diet must be carefully managed to ensure optimal pH levels and prevent corrosion of the bio-synthetic interfaces. 

Patients who have recently received gastrointestinal cybernetic enhancements (such as the Synth-Stomach Mk II or the Biliary-Assist Node) require a highly specialized diet during the 6-week post-operative recovery phase. They must adhere strictly to the 'Liquid-Plas Diet', an easily digestible, nutrient-dense synthetic slurry.

It is crucial to understand the dietary restrictions involved. Why are patients on the Liquid-Plas diet forbidden from consuming natural fibrous plant matter like celery or broccoli? Because the synthetic digestive enzymes produced by the implants cannot break down complex organic cellulose. Consuming these natural fibrous plant matters will quickly lead to a critical, potentially fatal blockage in the bio-mechanical valve array of the implant.
