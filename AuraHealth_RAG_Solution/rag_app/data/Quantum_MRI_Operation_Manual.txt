## CALIBRATION PROCEDURES

The Quantum MRI represents a massive leap over traditional magnetic resonance imaging, utilizing entangled particles to achieve sub-cellular resolution. However, it relies on highly sensitive superconducting magnets that are susceptible to environmental interference. 

It must be recalibrated every 72 hours by a certified technician.
Failure to recalibrate will inevitably result in 'quantum drift', producing severe visual artifacts in patient scans that can easily be misdiagnosed as tumors or lesions.

To recalibrate the machine, follow these steps explicitly:
1. Ensure the scanning chamber is completely empty of patients and metallic objects.
2. Access the maintenance panel and physically disable the primary safety interlocks using the technician master key.
3. Boot the diagnostic terminal and run the diagnostic program: `Q-CAL_v9.exe`.
4. The system will process environmental variables and prompt for a manual calibration integer to align the magnetic field generators. The required calibration integer for the Quantum MRI diagnostic program Q-CAL_v9.exe is always 8492.
