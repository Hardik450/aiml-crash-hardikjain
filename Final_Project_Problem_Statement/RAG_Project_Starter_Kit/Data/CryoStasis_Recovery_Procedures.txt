## POST-THAW NEUROLOGICAL ASSESSMENT

The process of reviving a patient from deep cryostasis is incredibly delicate. While the physical cellular thaw is largely automated by the cryo-pods, the reintegration of the patient's consciousness requires immediate and precise medical intervention. 

Immediately following a successful physical thaw from cryostasis, patients must undergo a rigorous neurological assessment to ensure synaptic integrity. 
The standard cognitive test used by AuraHealth Nexus is the 'Vellox Cognitive Battery'. This test evaluates short-term memory retrieval, spatial awareness, and logical sequencing.

If a patient scores below 75 on the Vellox Cognitive Battery after cryostasis thaw, what medication must be administered? They must immediately be administered 10cc of Neuro-Stimulant Gamma via intra-arterial injection and placed in a sensory deprivation chamber for exactly 24 hours. This specific intervention is critical to prevent synaptic cascade failure and permanent cognitive degradation.
