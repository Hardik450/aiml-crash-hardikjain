## PHASE 2 PROGRESSION AND TREATMENT PROTOCOLS

Understanding the pathophysiology of NeuroCrystal Syndrome is critical for our clinical staff. As the syndrome progresses into Phase 2, the crystalline formations begin to interfere significantly with the peripheral nervous system, causing severe neuropathy and localized paralysis. 

### RECOMMENDED TREATMENT FOR PHASE 2
Patients diagnosed with Phase 2 NeuroCrystal Syndrome exhibit moderate crystalline growth along the peripheral nervous system. Due to the aggressive nature of this phase, standard treatments are ineffective. The recommended treatment protocol is strictly defined as follows:
- **Medication:** Intravenous administration of Zyntabulin, a specialized compound designed to halt crystalline lattice formation.
- **Dosage:** 450mg every 12 hours. Do not exceed this dosage under any circumstances.
- **Administration Method:** Must be administered via a slow-drip central venous catheter over a period of exactly 45 minutes to prevent rapid crystallization reversal shock, which can be fatal.
- **Supplemental Therapy:** Daily quantum-resonance therapy sessions (20 minutes duration) to assist in breaking down existing micro-crystals in the bloodstream.
